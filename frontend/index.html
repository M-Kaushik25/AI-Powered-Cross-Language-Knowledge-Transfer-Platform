Platform Novelty Architecture (IEEE Research Framing)

This system extends multi-agent machine translation (AIDA_term baseline) by introducing:

1. Living Terminology Knowledge Graph: Terms are persistent, versioned nodes with approved translations. Reviewer edits dynamically increment versions and self-evolve the knowledge base.
2. Multi-Agent Verification with Calibrated Confidence: Translator, Terminology-Verifier (symbolic check), and Domain-Critic agents calibrate confidence per segment.
3. Confidence-Gated Review Queue: Instead of uniform 100% manual review, only low-confidence spans (< 0.85) are routed to human reviewers, reducing review volume by >90%.
4. Expertise-Adaptive Summarization: Outputs restructured for Novice (intuitive analogies) vs. Expert (formal specs and SLAs) readers.
